4. Prediction is not causation¶

The churn model ranks risk. The uplift model ranks expected incremental response. These rankings answer different business questions.

5. Commercial recommendation¶

  • Keep a randomised holdout before scaling.
  • Allocate intervention capacity by incremental uplift, not churn score alone.
  • Start with segments whose effect interval is positive and commercially meaningful.
  • Track cost per incremental retained customer, customer complaints and opt-outs.
  • Re-estimate after product, pricing or channel changes.

The synthetic findings demonstrate methodology and engineering capability; they are not claims about a real bank.
